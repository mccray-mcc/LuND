    02 — GREEN AREAS VS URBAN

    GREEN ISN'T
    THE SAME AS QUIET.

    47%OF GREATER LONDON IS GREEN COVER

    From 6% in the City of London to 68% in Bromley. Yet Harrow — over half green — has the largest share of residents exposed to road noise.

    Parks don't silence a city. Distance from traffic does. Step inside one and listen to London drop away.
